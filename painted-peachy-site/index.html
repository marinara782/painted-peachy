About Me

I'm Painted_Peachy. I started painting in 2026, and I hand-paint card binders and tokens for TCG players. Most of my work is League of Legends champions for the Riftbound crowd, plus Pokémon pieces for collectors.

Every binder cover is painted by hand and finished to hold up to real use at the table, so your collection looks as good on the outside as it does inside.
